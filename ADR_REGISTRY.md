# Governance

Every ADR must:

- have a unique identifier;
- describe the context;
- record the decision;
- document consequences;
- identify relevant alternatives where appropriate;
- reference related standards and documents;
- be reviewed before becoming Approved.

An ADR records a decision. It does not replace the governing standard or specification.
